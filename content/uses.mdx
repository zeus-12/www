## Raycast
<span className="text-muted-foreground">

    "Cause it deserves a section of its own and I clearly love yapping about it :)"

Raycast is one of those apps I didn’t like at first. I was happy with Alfred, and Raycast's root search wouldn't search files (update: they fixed this in the latest v, soo good), so finding a folder took multiple clicks. I'd uninstall it, hear good things about it on Reddit, give it another shot, get annoyed, & delete it. That cycle repeated a few times before it finally stuck.

What changed was spending time in the extension store. Raycast is much more than a launcher, every small annoyance either already had an extension, or a clean way to build one myself.

You can tell a lot of care went into the details. In the clipboard manager, pressing <Kbd>cmd</Kbd> + <Kbd>k</Kbd> shows extra actions like “Send to AI Chat” or “Save as snippet”. Small thing, but incredibly thoughtful.

Some things I use daily through Raycast:

- open projects directly in Cursor
- run bash scripts with keyboard shortcuts
- AI text workflows with diff and instant insert (was looking for a dedicated app for this, turns out Raycast does it for free with BYOK)
- caffeinate instead of amphetamine
- adding tasks / calendar scheduling
- color picker
- search tabs across spaces in Arc
- snippets & clipboard history
- emoji picker that’s actually fast (w semantic search)
- much better dictionary UX
- notes that hide during screen sharing
- window management
- kill process
- search files
- confetti 🎊
